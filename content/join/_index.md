# Join DS@GT ARC

**Fall 2026 update — September 16:** The Fall interest form is closed. If you submitted it but did not receive next steps, or want to ask about participation, see [joining and getting help](/faq/#joining-and-help).

## Getting started

1. Read the [ARC FAQ](/faq/), including the differences between Fall preparation and Spring research.
2. Review the [Fall 2026 page](/join/fall-2026/) for current participation guidance.
3. Join [DS@GT Slack](https://linktr.ee/datasciencegt) and the general `#applied-research-competitions` channel for updates, or use the [Contact page](/contact-us/) for help.
4. Explore [ARC Notes](https://notes.dsgt-arc.org/) for learning materials and [Publications](/publications/) for examples of team research.

## Spring 2027 applications

Spring has a separate application and team-formation process. Application dates and available tasks are not listed here yet; check general ARC announcements or contact leadership for current information. Working on a task in Fall does not guarantee placement on that task in Spring. Read [Spring research and team formation](/faq/#spring) for more context.

## Membership

ARC operates as a project group within the broader Data Science @ Georgia Tech student organization.

- **Who can join:** Georgia Tech students across programs, including online students, and alumni interested in participating. Resource and course eligibility must be confirmed separately.
- **Membership and resources:** see the [FAQ](/faq/#membership-and-resources) for participation routes, dues, and resource access.
- **Technical background:** Python, Git, and prior project-based experience are strongly recommended.
- **Details:** see the [FAQ](/faq/) for fuller expectations.

## Group structure and schedule

The group operates on a two-semester academic cycle.

- **Fall Interest Group & Preparation:** an introduction to competitive data science and applied research, with optional academic credit.
- **Spring Competition Research:** team-based competition and research work, with placements confirmed separately.

See [Academic credit](/faq/#academic-credit) for course guidance. Follow current course and organizer instructions for schedules and required work.
