# Fall 2026 Interest Group

The DS@GT ARC Fall Interest Group is an entry point for Georgia Tech students who want to learn more about applied machine learning, information retrieval, and competition-based research.

**[Submit the Fall 2026 Interest Form](https://go.dsgt-arc.org/survey-fall-2026)**

## Academic pathway

The ARC academic pathway is **one credit in Fall** and **three credits in Spring**. Course registration, coursework, and schedule details are still being finalized and will be shared when confirmed.

## Who should express interest?

- Georgia Tech students interested in applied research and collaborative technical projects
- Students with prior programming experience; familiarity with Python and Git is recommended
- New and returning ARC members, including online students

No prior research-competition experience is required.

## What happens next?

Submit the interest form so the team can share confirmed Fall details as they become available. You can also join the [DS@GT Slack](https://linktr.ee/datasciencegt) and the `#applied-research-competitions` channel for general ARC updates.

To learn more before signing up, review the [FAQ](/faq/), [Publications](/publications/), [Impact](/impact/), and [Contact](/contact-us/) pages.
