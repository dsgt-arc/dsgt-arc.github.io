# Fall 2026 Interest Group

The DS@GT ARC Fall Interest Group is an entry point for Georgia Tech students who want to learn more about applied machine learning, information retrieval, and competition-based research.

**September 16 update:** The Fall 2026 interest form is closed. If you submitted it but did not receive next steps, see [joining and getting help](/faq/#joining-and-help).

## Academic pathway

Fall participation does not require course registration. Fall 2026 has an optional one-credit CS 8903 course; ask course staff about registration and Spring credit options. See [Academic credit](/faq/#academic-credit).

Spring research has a separate application and team-formation process. Your Fall task does not reserve a Spring task or team. See [Spring research and team formation](/faq/#spring).

## Who is the interest group for?

- Georgia Tech students interested in applied research and collaborative technical projects
- Students with prior programming experience; familiarity with Python and Git is recommended
- New and returning ARC members, including online students

No prior research-competition experience is required.

## What happens next?

Ask the Fall organizers about onboarding or catch-up options through the [Contact page](/contact-us/) or the general `#applied-research-competitions` channel in [DS@GT Slack](https://linktr.ee/datasciencegt). Late participation must be confirmed by the organizers.

Public learning materials are available in [ARC Notes](https://notes.dsgt-arc.org/).

To learn more, review the [FAQ](/faq/), [Publications](/publications/), [Impact](/impact/), and [Contact](/contact-us/) pages.
